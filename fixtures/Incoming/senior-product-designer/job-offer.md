# Senior Product Designer

> Fictional demo offer. Northstar Labs is invented.

## Role

Shape accessible web experiences for workflow-heavy business products.

## Responsibilities

- Lead design from problem framing through launch and iteration.
- Conduct customer interviews and usability sessions.
- Create user flows, prototypes, interface designs, and specifications.
- Partner with product and engineering teams.
- Improve design systems and accessibility practices.
- Use product data and customer feedback to evaluate outcomes.

## Required experience

- Five or more years of product or UX design experience.
- Shipped web product work and a portfolio showing design reasoning.
- Responsive web design and design-system experience.
- Strong collaboration and communication skills.
- Familiarity with accessibility and inclusive design.
- Proficiency with Figma or a comparable design tool.

## Helpful experience

- B2B or workflow-heavy products.
- Qualitative research and product analytics.
- Mentoring designers or facilitating design critiques.
