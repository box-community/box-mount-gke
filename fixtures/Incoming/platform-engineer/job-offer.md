# Platform Engineer

> Fictional demo offer. Northstar Systems is invented.

## Role

Improve the reliability and developer experience of a cloud platform.

## Responsibilities

- Build and operate services on Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
- Maintain Kubernetes clusters and containerized workloads.
- Create reusable infrastructure with Terraform.
- Write automation and services in Go and Python.
- Improve observability with metrics, logs, tracing, and incident runbooks.
- Design and document REST APIs for internal platform services.
- Partner with application teams on secure, repeatable deployments.

## Required experience

- Four or more years of platform, DevOps, or site reliability engineering.
- Production experience with GCP, Kubernetes, Docker, and Terraform.
- Professional experience with Go or Python.
- Familiarity with Linux, networking, SQL, and REST APIs.
- Clear written communication and collaborative incident response.

## Helpful experience

- Prometheus and Grafana.
- GitHub Actions or another CI/CD system.
- Managed databases and PostgreSQL.
- Cost-aware capacity planning.
